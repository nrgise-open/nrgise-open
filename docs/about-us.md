## Our Other Tools

NRGISE Open is part of a broader toolbox of models, optimization methods, and software developed by our research group.

These Include:

- **NRGISE Trading:** Energy and balancing market models combined with multi-market trading algorithms. This allows us to simulate the operation and economics of large-scale storage and co-located PV + storage systems.
- [NRGISE.ONE](https://www.ise.fraunhofer.de/de/geschaeftsfelder/elektrische-energiespeicher/batterieintegration-und-betriebsfuehrung/integrierte-planung-und-steuerung-von-speichersystemen-nrgise-one.html): Integrated planning and control of storage-based energy systems, bringing the models and optimization methods used during system planning into real-world operation.
- [NRGISE.Plan](https://nrgise.ise.fraunhofer.de/nrgise/): The web-based planning application. Building on NRGISE Open and NRGISE Trading, it provides a convenient, user-friendly interface for configuring storage-based energy systems, running simulations, and performing technical and economic planning tasks with immediate access to results. 
- [SynPRO](https://synpro-lastprofile.de/): Scientifically validated synthetic load and generation profiles for heating, electricity and E-mobility.
- [OptiCharge](https://www.ise.fraunhofer.de/de/geschaeftsfelder/systemintegration/flexibilitaetsmanagement-von-energieanlagen/energiemanagement-prognosebasierte-optimierung.html): Optimized charging schedules for individual electric vehicles and fleets, supporting cost- and PV-optimized charging under grid constraints for uni- and bidirectional operation. Load and parking-time forecasts enable predictive planning of charging schedules.
  
Not all of these tools and models have their source available (yet). If you are interested in using them, learning more about our work, or collaborating with us, please [get in touch with us][get-in-touch].

## Working With Us

We are always interested in bringing our models and expertise into new projects. In particular, you can work with us on:

- **Simulation studies:** Techno-economic simulation and optimization of energy systems, ranging from commercial storage applications to EV charging infrastructure and large-scale battery storage. Our particular expertise includes optimal charging infrastructure and storage sizing for EV fleets, as well as market participation and profitability of utility-scale storage and co-located PV + storage projects.
- **Research collaborations:** Joint research on optimisation and control of local energy systems, and related topics.
- **Research proposals:** We regularly participate in publicly funded research projects and are interested in industry partners for upcoming proposals.

Have a project, research question, or idea? [Get in touch with us][get-in-touch].
